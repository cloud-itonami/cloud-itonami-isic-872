# Governance

This actor is part of the cloud-itonami Wave 4 (human-services) fleet.

- **Scope**: Coordination-only for residential care in mental health, intellectual disability, and substance abuse domains
- **Decision authority**: Governor (three HARD checks, permanent, un-overridable)
- **Allowed operations**: closed allowlist of 5 ops, all `:effect :propose`
- **Escalation**: safety-concern flagging, low confidence, governor rejection
